Scenario

Two facts contradict. The model picks one — sometimes wrong.

A customer service agent pulls account data from two sources. One says "Free tier," the other says "Premium." The agent must reconcile the contradiction — and picks arbitrarily.

The fix is collection forking: instead of querying live sources that may be out of sync, version all data collections to the same point in time. When the CRM and billing system both reflect the March 2024 snapshot, they agree — "Free tier" — and the contradiction disappears. The agent gives a deterministic, consistent answer every time.
